# Arc & Chapter Planning Engine (5 Planning Horizons)

## 1. Hierarchical Multi-Horizon Planning
To sustain web novels exceeding 1,000 chapters, planning is stratified across 5 horizons:
1. **Horizon 1: Immediate Execution (1–5 chapters)**: High-resolution scene plans, beat-by-beat dialogue intents, and micro-conflicts.
2. **Horizon 2: Near Horizon (5–30 chapters)**: Complete Arc blueprints, escalation milestones, midpoint reversals, and arc climaxes.
3. **Horizon 3: Medium Horizon (30–150 chapters)**: Saga architecture, major realm breakthroughs, continent-level faction shifts.
4. **Horizon 4: Long Horizon (150–500 chapters)**: Planar expansions, cosmic war arcs, core mystery breakthroughs.
5. **Horizon 5: Distant Horizon (500+ chapters)**: Ultimate ending state, cosmological transcendence, series climax.

## 2. Scene Blueprinting & Transition Continuity
Every scene specifies:
- : Character physical conditions, active injuries, location coordinates.
- : Resultant conditions, emotional changes, revealed secrets.
The engine validates consecutive scene transitions, automatically catching spatial teleportation and miraculous unexplained healing.
