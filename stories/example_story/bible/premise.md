# Test Story Premise: "The Ninefold Starlit Dao"

## Core Premise
In a world where classical Qi cultivation has degenerated into rigid dogmatic sect politics, **Lin Chen**, a mortal youth cast out from an auxiliary branch of the declining **Verdant Cloud Sect**, stumbles upon an ancient subterranean vault. Within, he unearths the dormant remnant of the **Starlit Sovereign**—an ancient civilization that harnessed cosmic stellar currents rather than earthly ley lines. Bound to an evolving astral compass that tracks karmic promises, Lin Chen must navigate ruthless sect trials, hidden assassins, and rising planar fissures while uncovering why the Starlit Empire was eradicated ten thousand years ago.

## Themes
* Merit and adaptability versus aristocratic bloodline dogmatism.
* The crushing weight of secrets and historical erasure.
* The consequences of ambition and karmic debt.

## Cardinal World Rules
1. **The Law of Tribulation:** Breakthroughs past Foundation Establishment attract heavenly tribulations proportional to karmic deviation.
2. **The Epistemic Seal:** Ancient star techniques are lethal if divulged to cultivators who have not unlocked stellar perception.
3. **No Resurrection:** The physical death of a soul core is irreversible; only echoes and memory fragments survive.
