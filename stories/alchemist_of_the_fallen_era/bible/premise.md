# Story Bible: The Alchemist of the Fallen Era

## 1. Core Premise
When ancient subterranean seals rupture beneath the modern world, dormant primordial miasma surges into the atmosphere, triggering violent biological and soul mutations across a fraction of humanity. While society implodes into bloody warlord fiefdoms and feral chimera zones, **Arthur Vance**—an overlooked, self-deprecating part-time librarian who meticulously memorized every book in the municipal archives simply because he felt it was his duty—discovers that modern biology cannot comprehend the apocalypse, but forgotten 14th-century hermetic alchemy can. 

Armed with a torn vellum leaf from an ancient codex and an encyclopedic understanding of anatomy, poisons, and catalysts, Arthur races across a collapsing metropolis to rescue his chronically ill younger sister, **Eleanor**, before escaping to a secluded mountain bunker where he begins his rise as the clandestine Alchemist of the Fallen Era.

---

## 2. World & Environmental Rules
1. **The Awakening of the Primordial Veins:** The miasma is not radiation or a virus; it is the chaotic vitality of Earth's prehistoric epoch, dense with primordial soul-force.
2. **The Mutation Paradox:** Directly absorbing raw miasma or ingesting mutant beast marrow grants explosive physical power, but steadily erodes human sanity, causing cancer-like flesh distortions and madness.
3. **The Necessity of Alchemy:** Only through rigorous extraction, purification, and soul-catalysis can beast organs and primordial herbs be distilled into safe, stable elixirs.
4. **The Law of Reversibility:** Once a human's soul fully mutates into a feral beast core, their humanity is irrevocably lost.

## 3. Narrative Pacing & Serialization Parameters
* **Total Target Scope:** ~500 chapters
* **Target Chapter Length:** 1,500–2,000 words (structured in 2–3 sharp scene beats)
* **Target Arc Length:** 30–50 chapters per arc (approximately 10–12 major arcs)
* **Tone:** Grimdark Survival, Hard Progression, Scientific/Alchemical Deduction, High Tension, Deep Sibling Loyalty
