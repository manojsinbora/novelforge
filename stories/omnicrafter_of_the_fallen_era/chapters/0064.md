# Chapter 64: The Ashen Covenant

The southern rim of the Yellowstone Caldera was a nightmare of boiling sulfur pools, black volcanic glass, and plumes of superheated steam that hissed from fissures in the snow.

*Day 17 // 6:30 AM.*

From the snow-covered crest of a granite pinnacle overlooking Obsidian Ridge at seven thousand eight hundred feet elevation, Arthur Vance adjusted the brass vernier knobs of his surveyor’s transit telescope.

Beside him lay Naomi Tsosie, Captain Rachel Cross, and Eleanor, their white camouflage snow-capes blending seamlessly into the three-foot snowdrifts. 

Through the optical crosshairs, two miles north across a frozen alpine plateau, the fortress of the Ashen Covenant rose like a demonic citadel carved from solid obsidian: **Project Tartarus**.

Built forty years before the Collapse by the Department of Energy, Project Tartarus was an industrial complex of immense proportions. 

Anchored into a sheer five-hundred-foot cliff of black volcanic basalt stood six massive reinforced-concrete wellhead bunkers, housing the master valve trees of the deepest geothermal boreholes ever drilled on the continent. Above the bunkers loomed a labyrinth of thirty-inch insulated steam mains, cyclone steam separators, and cooling towers, venting deafening plumes of geothermal steam that cast long, sulfur-yellow shadows across the snow.

Enclosing the complex was a formidable warlord redoubt: 

A twenty-foot-high perimeter wall constructed from stacked basalt blocks and jagged slabs of natural volcanic obsidian. Mounted in firing embrasures along the wall were four pre-Collapse heavy water-cooled machine guns and two high-angle mortars firing forty-pound spheres of chilled volcanic glass packed with black powder.

Patrolling the perimeter were the fanatical sentries of the Ashen Covenant: over two hundred fire-mutated zealots. 

Through the transit lens, Arthur examined their physical physiology: their skin had undergone severe thermal-silicate mutation, transformed into thick, charcoal-black scales encrusted with vitreous quartz and iron slag. They wore heavy asbestos smocks and brass respirators, carrying modified industrial flame-throwers that fired liquid sulfur slurry at eighteen hundred degrees Fahrenheit.

Naomi Tsosie handed Arthur a leather-bound journal recovered from a dead Covenant scout two weeks prior:

"Ignis wasn't always a madman, Arthur," Tsosie explained, her voice tight with sorrow. "In the first years after the Collapse, he tried to use Tartarus to generate power for the mountain refugees. But as the geothermal tremors worsened and the miasma seeped into the wellheads, he became obsessed with the ancient First Dynasty runes carved into the deep basalt. He came to believe that humanity’s reliance on machines was a mortal sin, and that the First Dynasty were 'jailers' who trapped the Earth’s primordial divine fire in crystalline chains. His cult practices the 'Baptism of Slag'—submerging their limbs in boiling volcanic mineral pools until their flesh vitrifies into stone."

"A classic sociological pathology," Arthur remarked, scanning the fanatic hymns transcribed in the journal. "When complex technological systems fail without understandable explanations, primitive minds construct theological narratives to justify destruction. They worship the entropy that they cannot engineer."

Arthur redirected his telescope toward the courtyard.

Parked inside the main courtyard were three monstrous combat vehicles:

**The Obsidian Steam Crawlers.**

Each vehicle was a sixty-ton tracked earthmover encased in three-inch-thick slabs of natural volcanic obsidian fused to steel boiler plate. Mounted in traversing steel turrets on the roofs were high-pressure geothermal steam cannons, connected via flexible armored steam hoses to the facility’s pressurized steam mains.

"Look at those steam cannons, Arthur," Naomi Tsosie whispered, her breath freezing on her sheepskin collar. "Those are pre-Collapse casing-wash lances. They operate on fifteen hundred PSI supercritical steam at six hundred degrees Fahrenheit. A thirty-second burst will strip the flesh from a squad of soldiers and melt the engine block of an armored truck at eighty yards."

Captain Cross swept her binoculars across the central drilling apron.

"What about the demolition charges, Naomi?" Cross asked quietly.

Tsosie pointed her gloved finger toward the base of Wellhead Number One—a colossal fifty-foot Christmas-tree valve manifold fabricated from forged alloy steel, anchored directly over the primary hydrothermal conduit.

Lashed around the four master gate valves with heavy chains were twenty wooden crates stamped with pre-Collapse military markings: **Composition H-6 / Hexogen High Explosives**.

A heavy, braided detonation cord ran from the charges up into the fortified concrete command bunker atop the cliff.

"Twenty thousand pounds of military hexogen," Tsosie said, her voice shaking with quiet fury. "Ignis has wired them to a central electronic timer inside the Tartarus control room. When that timer hits zero at noon tomorrow, the charges will fire simultaneously, blowing the wellhead trees clean off the bedrock."

Arthur Vance did not blink. 

He drew his number-two drafting pencil from behind his ear, flipping to a fresh grid-lined page in his leather ledger:

"The physical parameters of the hydrothermal caprock are precise," Arthur stated in his calm, measured cadence. "Supercritical geothermal fluid at depth exists at a temperature of seven hundred and twenty degrees Fahrenheit and a lithostatic pressure of three thousand eight hundred PSI. It is held in liquid phase solely by the mechanical confinement of the twelve-thousand-foot basalt caprock."

He wrote out the thermodynamic equations:

$$\Delta P_{\text{instant}} = 3,800 \text{ PSI} \rightarrow 14.7 \text{ PSI (Atmospheric)}$$

$$\Delta V = \frac{v_{\text{steam}}}{v_{\text{water}}} \approx 1,600\text{x volumetric expansion ratio}$$

"If twenty thousand pounds of hexogen shears those wellheads," Arthur explained, "the sudden depressurization will trigger an instantaneous, catastrophic phreatomagmatic chain reaction. The superheated liquid in thirty cubic miles of subterranean aquifer will flash into steam within zero point four seconds, expanding sixteen hundred times in volume. The resulting kinetic shockwave will shatter the remaining caprock, venting the magma chamber into the atmosphere within ninety seconds."

Eleanor pressed her fingertips into the snow. 

Her clear emerald eyes widened as her harmonized Starlight Sovereign resonance penetrated deep through the mountain strata:

"Artie," Eleanor whispered. "The magma dome is rising. The ground beneath Obsidian Ridge is swelling by one point two millimeters every hour. And inside the central bunker... I can feel Ignis. His body is burning with molten silicate. He's standing over the detonator, waiting for the sun to reach its zenith tomorrow."

Beside Ignis, Eleanor’s starlight sensed a second, terrifying presence:

"And there's a beast guarding the wellhead gates," she added softly. "A giant nine feet tall... his skin is molten slag and obsidian glass. His heart beats with volcanic fire."

"**Slag-Hound Kenneth**," Tsosie growled, her dark eyes flashing with hatred. "Ignis’s chief executioner. He dipped his own arms into molten rhyolite during their covenant initiation. His limbs are encrusted with four inches of natural volcanic glass. Bullets can't pierce him; fire only makes him stronger. He wields twin pneumatic slag-breakers that can crush an armored crawler in half."

Arthur Vance adjusted his wire-rimmed spectacles, his expression remaining completely unperturbed.

"Obsidian is a natural volcanic glass formed by the rapid quenching of felsic lava," Arthur noted, analyzing the mineralogical properties. "It consists of seventy percent silicon dioxide ($\text{SiO}_2$) with a Mohs hardness of five point five. While exceptionally hard and impervious to thermal scorch, it possesses zero plastic ductility."

He retrieved a sharp nodule of black obsidian from the snow, holding it up to the light:

"Like all amorphous glasses, obsidian is catastrophically vulnerable to **Cryogenic Thermal Shock and Brittle Micro-Fracture Propagation**," Arthur stated. "When subjected to a sudden, extreme temperature differential of six hundred degrees Fahrenheit, thermal contraction generates localized tensile stresses that exceed its ultimate tensile strength of seven thousand PSI by a factor of eight. The glass has no crystalline grain boundaries to arrest dislocation movement. It will shatter into microscopic dust."

He pointed his pencil at the Griffith crack propagation formula sketched in his ledger:

$$\sigma_f = \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot E \cdot \gamma}{\pi \cdot a}}$$

Arthur pulled a small portable propane torch from his belt, heating the obsidian nodule until it glowed dull cherry red at four hundred and fifty degrees. 

He then unstoppered a small thermos flask of liquid nitrogen, letting a single droplet of minus-three-hundred-degree liquid fall upon the glowing glass.

*SNAP-SHATTER!*

With a sharp, gunshot-like crack, the obsidian nodule exploded into hundreds of glittering, microscopic shards that peppered the snow like black sand.

Captain Cross and Naomi Tsosie stared at the pulverized glass in stunned silence.

"That," Arthur Vance said calmly, "is how we will destroy their sixty-ton crawlers, their gates, and Slag-Hound Kenneth."

He looked at Captain Cross, Sergeant Major Bradley, and Naomi Tsosie:

"Here is our operational plan for Obsidian Ridge:

"First: we will not engage in a static artillery exchange against their steam cannons. Instead, we will deploy a synchronized **Cryo-Thermal Inversion and Rapid Breaching** assault.

"We will utilize *The Iron Vanguard’s* steam generator car to power Naomi’s heavy steam crawler as an armored ice-ram, fitted with a high-pressure liquid nitrogen cryogenic spray manifold. When the crawler impacts the obsidian perimeter gate, the nitrogen will flash-freeze the glass armor to minus three hundred degrees Fahrenheit, while the steam lance delivers an immediate eight-hundred-degree thermal shock. The obsidian gate will disintegrate under its own internal stress."

Bradley grinned fiercely, racking the bolt of his rifle. "And Slag-Hound Kenneth?"

"I will neutralize Kenneth personally using the newly completed **Chimera Frame Mark V**," Arthur answered calmly. "His slag armor will be embrittled and shattered via high-pressure kinetic impact. Once the courtyard is secured, Company B riflemen will seal the wellheads with Naomi’s weighted barite mud, while Eleanor and I infiltrate the Tartarus control bunker to disarm the master detonator."

Arthur clicked open his gold-plated Elgin pocket watch.

*8:45 AM.*

"Countdown to solstice detonation: twenty-seven hours, fifteen minutes," Arthur recorded in his ledger. "Let us return to the commune roundhouse and finalize assembly of the Mark V."

The reconnaissance team strapped their webbed rawhide snowshoes back onto their insulated boots, beginning the two-mile return trek across the silent alpine meadows.

The wind had died down, leaving the high mountain spruce forests in breathless, pristine quiet. But beneath their feet, the faint, rhythmic harmonic thrumming of the destabilized caldera vibrated through the snowpacks every twelve minutes, dislodging powdery avalanches from the high granite crags.

Eleanor walked beside Arthur, her golden starlight aura filtering the sulfur fumes from the air.

"Artie," Eleanor asked softly, her breath clouding in the cold. "Do you think Ignis was ever a good scientist? Before the fire took his mind?"

"Competence in physics does not confer psychological immunity against existential trauma," Arthur answered in his calm, reflective cadence. "When confronted with systemic catastrophe, a mind lacking philosophical discipline seeks solace in mystical fatalism. Dr. Caldwell ceased to be an engineer the moment he surrendered to the fire instead of measuring it."

By 10:15 AM, the team crossed the wooden perimeter gates of Jackson Hole.

In the commune yard, Master Kovacs and Naomi Tsosie’s drilling crews were already loading two heavy tanker trailers with twenty thousand pounds of high-density barite mud, rigging high-pressure mud pumps to the auxiliary power takes of the steam crawlers.

Arthur walked into the warm, brightly lit machine shop of Car Three, taking off his heavy snow-cape.

He picked up a clean linen cloth, polished his spectacles, and retrieved his number-two pencil:

*Reconnaissance completed: Obsidian Ridge mapped.*  
*Target: Project Tartarus Geothermal Complex.*  
*Hexogen demolition charges located at Wellhead One.*  
*Demolition countdown: 25 hours, 45 minutes.*  
*Machine shop status: Ready for Mark V assembly.*

Arthur looked at the precision lathe, where a block of titanium-niobium alloy and a vacuum canister of silica aerogel sat ready.

"Joseph," Arthur said to Kovacs. "Light the electric furnace. We assemble the Mark V."
