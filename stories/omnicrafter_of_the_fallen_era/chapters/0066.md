# Chapter 66: The Battle of Obsidian Ridge

The blizzard howling across the volcanic plateau was black with falling rhyolitic ash and sulfuric cinders.

*Day 17 // 8:15 PM.*

Two miles south of Project Tartarus, *The Iron Vanguard* stood locked to the rails on an abandoned pre-Collapse industrial siding. Its sixteen-cylinder diesels rumbled at low idle, feeding forty thousand watts of direct-current electrical power and high-pressure steam through insulated umbilical cables into the forward assault line.

Rumbling across the frozen volcanic ash at fifteen miles per hour was the Shoshone Commune’s heavy sixty-ton steam crawler tractor, christened *The Thunder-Beast*. 

The crawler was a marvel of rugged mountain engineering: propelled by twin continuous steel tracks four feet wide, its planetary final drive gearing operated at a massive forty-eight-to-one reduction ratio, generating ninety thousand pounds of tractive drawbar pull. Reinforced by Master Kovacs with welded biogenic steel plates, the crawler was driven by Naomi Tsosie, while Arthur Vance stood anchored to the open exterior observer’s deck behind an armored ballistic windshield. 

Locked around Arthur’s right arm and torso was the newly minted **Chimera Frame Mark V**. Its vacuum-jacketed silica aerogel lining shielded his body from the freezing mountain gale, while its twin accumulator pressure dials read a rock-solid **Six Thousand PSI**. 

On his left forearm, the Sovereign Aegis buckler hummed at five hundred and twenty-eight hertz, connected via a flexible star-iron coaxial lead to Eleanor’s starlight resonance bus.

To the east along a parallel basalt ridge, Sergeant Major Hank Bradley led eight elite snipers of Company B on snowshoes, positioning their Barrett .50-caliber anti-material rifles in snow trenches to provide covering fire against the fortress parapets.

Suddenly, two blinding crimson flares shot into the night sky from the obsidian walls of Project Tartarus.

*KABOOOOOM! KABOOOOOM!*

The fortress gates swung wide with a screech of unlubricated iron hinges. 

Roaring down the volcanic slope came the Covenant’s armored vanguard: three monstrous sixty-ton **Obsidian Steam Crawlers**, their heavy steel tracks throwing up rooster-tails of black ash and frozen snow. 

Mounted in armored steel turrets atop each crawler were high-pressure casing-wash steam cannons. The turrets traversed toward *The Thunder-Beast*, their brass nozzles glowing dull orange in the dark.

*HSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS!*

From the three enemy turrets, supersonic jets of supercritical geothermal steam erupted across the snowfields:

**One Thousand Five Hundred PSI at Six Hundred Degrees Fahrenheit.**

The thermal shockwave was apocalyptic. 

The superheated steam jets vaporized five feet of snowpack instantly, creating a roaring, scalding whiteout wall of boiling water vapor that tore across the plateau at eight hundred feet per second, capable of stripping the flesh from bone in a fraction of a second.

"Eleanor! Thermodynamic condensation vortex!" Arthur commanded through his throat microphone.

Eleanor Vance stood on the forward catwalk of *The Iron Vanguard* two miles back, her hands extended toward the ridge.

Her emerald eyes blazed with brilliant, pure golden starlight. 

She focused her harmonized Mid-Tier Sovereign resonance directly into the path of the approaching steam jets, projecting an immense geometric mandala of negative electrostatic potential across the front of *The Thunder-Beast*.

The physics of phase equilibrium reacted instantaneously.

$$\Delta H_{\text{condensation}} = -2,260 \text{ kJ/kg}$$

The Covenant gunners screamed in shock, frantically trying to clear their steam valves.

"Now, Naomi!" Arthur ordered. "Engage the Cryo-Thermal Spray Lance! Target lead crawler's frontal glacis!"

Naomi Tsosie slammed the crawler's hydraulic drive levers forward, closing the range to forty feet. 

Mounted on the nose of *The Thunder-Beast* were two rigid stainless-steel spray nozzles fed by high-pressure Dewar flasks.

With the agility of a coiled spring, Arthur vaulted over the safety railing of *The Thunder-Beast*.

His vulcanized rubber boots carried him across the ash-covered snow in three blinding strides. He leaped onto the tread guards of the stripped Covenant crawler, vaulting directly onto its exposed boiler deck.

Arthur did not pause.

He vaulted to the ground, raised the Sovereign Aegis buckler on his left arm, and signaled Bradley's sniper team:

"Bradley! Target crawlers two and three! Armor-piercing incendiary volley on my mark!"

*THUMP-THUMP-THUMP-THUMP-THUMP!*

Within twenty seconds, both vehicles burst into flames, their mutant crews abandoning the hatches and fleeing in terror toward the fortress walls.

Naomi Tsosie gunned *The Thunder-Beast’s* throttle, surging up the slope behind Arthur.

The sixty-ton Shoshone crawler lowered its heavy biogenic steel ram, slamming directly into the twenty-foot obsidian perimeter gates of Project Tartarus:

*CRASH-BOOOOOOM!*

The gates collapsed inward under sixty tons of crushing momentum, showering the courtyard in black glass rubble and twisted iron bars.

Company B riflemen and Shoshone scouts poured through the breach in flawless tactical formation, disarming forty stunned Covenant zealots and stripping them of their liquid-sulfur flamethrowers within four minutes.

*10:30 PM.*
