# Chapter 85: The Siege of Council Bluffs

Dusk settled over the frozen plains of western Iowa in a bruised palette of deep indigo and blood-orange fire.

*Day 27 // 5:10 PM.*

The wind screamed across the flat corn stubble, driving sheets of frozen sleet against the timber-and-sandbag barricades of the Council Bluffs railhead. The temperature had plunged to minus eighteen degrees Fahrenheit, but the air around the perimeter was suffocatingly hot with the acrid stench of burning lignite coal, cordite exhaust, and boiling machine grease.

One thousand yards east of the railhead fence, Baron Malakor’s mechanized vanguard surged forward in a three-column assault formation.

Twenty armored steam technicals, their rusted boilerplate cowcatchers throwing up white wakes of snow, roared across the fields at twenty-five miles per hour. Behind them came forty armed motorcycle outriders firing submachine guns from sidecars, followed by five hundred Gary Reapers advancing on foot beneath the deafening, earth-shaking tread of the **Colossus of Gary**.

The sixty-foot walking dreadnought strode at the center of the iron wave. 

Its twin vertical fire-tube boilers roared like twin blast furnaces, venting fiery sparks and black smoke sixty feet into the darkening sky. Its two forty-foot excavator flail arms were lowered, their heavy six-foot circular buzzsaws and rotating barbed chains spinning at five hundred revolutions per minute with an ear-splitting mechanical shriek:

*SKREEEEEEE-CLATTER-CLATTER-SKREEEEE!*

Standing atop the sandbag revetment guarding the main railroad switch, Arthur Vance watched the approaching army through his brass naval telescope.

Beside him stood Captain Rachel Cross, her carbine resting on the parapet, flanked by Sergeant Major Hank Bradley and thirty veteran riflemen of Company B. To Arthur’s right, Eleanor Vance stood with her hands clasped gently before her, her Mid-Tier Sovereign core humming with quiet, concentrated power, projecting a translucent golden aura that kept the bitter sleet from frosting their goggles.

In the passenger terminal behind the revetments, four hundred civilian refugees from the Missouri River farming communes huddled together. Eleanor had walked among them only twenty minutes prior, laying hands on crying infants and frightened elders, wrapping the entire interior of the brick station in a warm, soothing field of 528 Hz starlight.

"Do not fear the sound of their cannons," Eleanor had whispered to an elderly grandmother trembling over her grandchildren. "The Archivist has calculated every trajectory, every bolt, and every pound of force. Their machines cannot conquer the truth of the universe."

Back on the firing line, Arthur Vance adjusted the shoulder harness of the newly forged **Chimera Frame Mark VI**.

The tandem hydraulic intensifier on his chest hissed softly at seven thousand five hundred PSI; the dual nitrogen jump thrusters on his hips rested fully charged at eight thousand PSI. The vanadium-alloyed PDC diamond chisel on his right arm caught the dying orange light of the western horizon like a facet of polished black obsidian.

"Tactical ballistic analysis," Arthur announced calmly, opening his leather ledger and unclicking his mechanical pencil. "Hostile assault velocity: twenty-four miles per hour (thirty-five point two feet per second). Enemy technicals are armed with water-cooled twelve-point-seven-millimeter Browning machine guns and improvised five-inch unguided solid-fuel incendiary rockets. Rocket propellant composition: coarse ammonium perchlorate and asphaltum binders exhibiting a burn rate of zero point six inches per second."

Captain Cross racked the bolt of her precision rifle. "They're entering the outer wire entanglements, Arthur. Request permission to open fire with the *Prometheus* rail battery."

"Permission granted," Arthur replied evenly. "Target prioritization: lead technical vehicles on the northern and southern flanks. Ballistic calculation for the four-inch star-iron rail howitzers mounted on Flatcar Three:"

$$\text{Range: } R = 950 \text{ yards} = 2,850 \text{ ft}$$

$$\text{Muzzle Velocity: } v_0 = 1,450 \text{ ft/s}, \quad \text{Elevation Angle: } \theta = 3.8^\circ$$

$$\text{Time of Flight: } t = \frac{R}{v_0 \cdot \cos\theta} \approx 1.97 \text{ seconds}$$

"Apply two mils of left windage correction to compensate for the eighteen-knot northwest crosswind," Arthur instructed Bradley over the field telephone. "Battery Three: fire on my mark."

Arthur checked his gold pocket watch.

*5:14:22 PM.*

"Mark."

*KABOOOOOM! KABOOOOOM!*

The twin four-inch star-iron howitzers mounted aboard the *Prometheus*’s heavy artillery flatcar erupted in a violent flash of yellow flame and white nitrocellulose smoke.

Two fifty-pound high-explosive fragmentation shells screamed across the frozen fields. Exactly one point nine seven seconds later, they struck the lead technicals of the northern and southern assault columns with devastating precision:

*CRACK-BOOM! CRACK-BOOM!*

The two lead armored trucks disintegrated in violent geysers of shrapnel, burning kerosene, and flying tire rubber. The shockwaves hurled the flanking motorcycle outriders into the deep snowdrifts, throwing the raider assault columns into chaotic disarray.

Simultaneously, the water-cooled machine guns of Company B opened up along the sandbag perimeter:

*TAT-TAT-TAT-TAT-TAT-TAT-TAT!*

A relentless, interlocking curtain of armor-piercing seven-point-six-two-millimeter rounds swept across the snowfields. The concentrated fire ripped through the un-annealed boilerplate armor of four more technicals, puncturing radiator cores and detonating ammunition bins.

On the southern flank, a desperate squad of twelve Gary Reaper sappers attempted to crawl through an irrigation culvert carrying magnetic sticky bombs to disable the rail switch.

Sergeant Major Bradley swung a sixty-thousand-candlepower star-iron searchlight across the frozen ditch:

"Culvert hostiles spotted!" Bradley bellowed, directing two riflemen with semi-automatic carbines.

*PHUT-PHUT-PHUT!*

Within six seconds, the sappers' sticky bombs detonated in the ditch beneath their own bodies, collapsing the culvert and sealing the flank.

Aboard the bridge wing of the Colossus, Baron Malakor watched his flank columns stall in a fury of incandescent rage.

He seized his pneumatic bullhorn, his cybernetic red ocular lens glowing with feral madness:

"Cowards! Scum!" Malakor roared at his surviving forces. "Ignore the rifle fire! Follow the Colossus! Flails down! Tear their gate off its hinges!"

Malakor slammed the hydraulic control valves on his console.

The sixty-foot walking juggernaut accelerated its stride, surging forward at one point four miles per hour. Each thunderous stomp of its thirty-foot walking shoes crushed frozen soil, barbed wire, and farm fences into splinters:

*KER-THUMP! KER-THUMP!*

The machine reached the outer perimeter six hundred yards out. 

Its twin revolving flail arms swept forward in a massive horizontal arc. The forty-foot steel beams, swinging heavy barbed-chain flails at five hundred RPM, struck the allied perimeter’s outer timber palisade:

*CRASH-SHATTER-SPLINTER!*

Twelve-inch-thick oak logs were obliterated into kindling and flying splinters in a single stroke. 

Behind the flails, the machine’s center cupola elevated its twin 152-millimeter naval siege mortars, aiming directly at the main entrance of the Council Bluffs passenger terminal where the civilian refugees were huddled.

"Die in your hole, clerk!" Malakor shrieked, pulling the firing lanyards.

*BOOOOOOM! BOOOOOOM!*

Two colossal eighty-pound mortar shells leaped from the twin muzzles, screaming downward on a steep parabolic trajectory directly toward the terminal roof.

Arthur Vance did not step back. 

He looked toward Eleanor. "Eleanor. Maximum harmonic interception."

Eleanor Vance stepped out onto the apex of the central rail switch, her hair whipping in the sub-zero wind.

She closed her eyes, placing both hands flat against the star-iron rail beneath her boots.

*SHUUUUUUU-VOOOOOOOOOM!*

A blinding, breathtaking pillar of pure golden 528 Hz starlight erupted from Eleanor’s core, ascending two hundred feet into the winter twilight before mushrooming outward in a shimmering, solid dome of coherent energy.

The two eighty-pound mortar shells slammed into the starlight shield one hundred feet above the terminal roof:

*VZZZZZT-CLACK!*

The kinetic collision sounded not like an explosion, but like the striking of an ancient bronze temple bell. 

The coherent harmonic field absorbed eighty-four thousand foot-pounds of kinetic energy in zero point zero two seconds. The nose fuses of both shells were instantly stripped of their electrostatic firing charge. The heavy iron casings halted dead in mid-air, their momentum reduced to zero, and plummeted harmlessly into the snowdrifts beside the tracks like two spent rocks.

Eleanor exhaled a soft, steady breath, her violet eyes shining with serene majesty. "The sanctuary holds, Arthur."

"The sanctuary holds," Arthur agreed quietly.

Across the field, the Colossus of Gary was now less than two hundred yards away, striding through the shattered outer palisade. 

Its left thirty-foot walking shoe lifted into the air, swinging forward on its colossal forty-ton eccentric cam, preparing to crush the central rail switch and smash the *Prometheus*’s pilot car.

Arthur Vance unbuttoned his wool greatcoat, letting the heavy garment drop cleanly onto the sandbags. 

He stood in his tailored charcoal waistcoat, linen shirt, and wool trousers, the **Chimera Frame Mark VI** gleaming on his limbs with lethal readiness.

He pulled his gold pocket watch from his vest, clicked the hunter case open with his left thumb, and watched the golden second hand sweep toward the twelve:

*5:21:48 PM... 49... 50... 51... 52...*

"Eccentric cam cycle duration: seven point zero six seconds," Arthur calculated aloud with clinical precision. "Phase angle: ninety degrees. The left walking shoe is reaching maximum elevation of six feet above grade. The six-inch high-pressure steam distribution header is exposed for precisely three point four seconds."

Arthur snapped the pocket watch shut and slipped it securely into his waistcoat pocket.

He looked at Captain Cross. "Captain Cross. Cease fire on the central sector."

Cross lowered her rifle, staring at him. "Arthur... what are you doing?"

"I am going to dismantle Baron Malakor's machine," Arthur answered deadpan.

Arthur crouched low on the sandbag revetment, flexing his knees.

The dual nitrogen jump thrusters on his hips clicked into full eight-thousand-PSI lock.

*KER-BLAST!*

An explosive detonation of compressed gas erupted beneath Arthur’s boots.

The Chimera Frame Mark VI launched Arthur Vance twenty-five feet straight into the freezing twilight sky. He soared over the shattered palisade, over the screaming flail arms, his body cutting a clean arc through the snow-choked air directly toward the belly of the Colossus of Gary.

Mid-flight, Arthur activated the magnetic soles of his boots.

As his upward momentum peaked at twenty-five feet, Arthur extended his legs, aiming his boots directly at the Colossus's left walking gimbal—a massive four-foot steel structural ring encrusted with frost.

*CLANG-THUMP!*

The eight-hundred-pound neodymium magnetic pads locked onto the steel plating with an unyielding metallic grip.

Arthur Vance landed smoothly upon the moving flank of the walking dreadnought, sixty feet above the frozen ground. The immense machine shuddered beneath his feet with every titanic step, its roaring boilers venting searing plumes of steam only ten feet above his head.

Clinging to the vibrating steel flank with magnetic boots and his left hydraulic gripping clamp, Arthur looked down between the churning gears of the walking cam.

Directly below him ran the primary six-inch high-pressure steam distribution header—glowing with dark maroon thermal heat beneath a crust of burned coal oil.

Arthur leveled the **Chimera Frame Mark VI**'s vanadium-alloyed diamond chisel directly against the master steam manifold flange.

The tandem hydraulic intensifier hummed with lethal readiness at **Seven Thousand Five Hundred PSI**.

"Target acquired," Arthur whispered into his throat microphone. "Initiating mechanical demobilization."

The siege of Council Bluffs was over.

The execution of mechanical physics had begun.
