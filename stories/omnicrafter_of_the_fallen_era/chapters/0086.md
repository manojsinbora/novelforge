# Chapter 86: Dismantling the Colossus

Sixty feet above the frozen prairie, clinging to the vibrating steel flank of the Bucyrus-Erie walking chassis, Arthur Vance was an island of absolute stillness amidst an inferno of steam and kinetic fury.

*Day 27 // 5:23 PM.*

The roar of the Colossus of Gary’s twin vertical boilers was a physical assault on the senses. The massive three-inch hull plates trembled at twenty-four cycles per second as the forty-ton walking cams churned beneath Arthur’s boots. Searing plumes of black coal soot and superheated moisture screamed from overhead bypass flues, whipped into swirling vortexes by the sub-zero Iowa blizzard.

Yet beneath his full-face chemical respirator, Arthur’s breathing remained calm, measured, and rhythmic: exactly twelve respirations per minute.

His magnetic boots held him anchored to the four-foot load-bearing gimbal ring of the left walking leg with eight hundred pounds of switchable neodymium adhesion. Directly before him ran the primary six-inch forged-iron steam distribution header, vibrating violently under two hundred and forty PSI of superheated steam, pulsing fluid power to the colossal hydraulic cylinders that actuated the left thirty-foot walking shoe.

Arthur pulled his gold pocket watch from his waistcoat, clicked the hunter case open, and watched the sweep of the second hand against the glowing dial:

*5:23:42 PM.*

"Target: Left Hydraulic Cam Steam Header," Arthur whispered into his throat microphone, recording the parameters in his mind. "Operating pressure: two hundred and forty PSI. Material: ductile nodular iron, wall thickness: zero point three seven five inches. Fastening: eight one-inch Grade-Five carbon steel flange bolts under sixty foot-pounds of torque."

"Arthur! You've got hostiles on the crane catwalk above you!" Captain Rachel Cross’s voice crackled through his radio earpiece from the railhead two hundred yards away. "Two machine gunners in the upper gallery are leaning over the safety rail!"

Arthur did not look up.

High above on the gantry, two Gary Reaper gunners spotted the charcoal-suited figure clinging to their machine's underbelly. They raised their heavy twelve-gauge automatic shotguns, firing a deafening salvo of lead buckshot straight down at Arthur's head:

*BLAM! BLAM! BLAM!*

Arthur brought up the **Sovereign Aegis induction buckler** on his left forearm.

*VZZZZT-CLANG-SPARK!*

The Sovereign Aegis’s electromagnetic induction coils flared with intense sapphire-blue light. 

The cloud of high-velocity lead pellets struck the invisible 528 Hz standing wave barrier, losing all kinetic momentum in zero point zero zero three seconds. Instead of piercing Arthur’s coat, the harvested kinetic energy—over six thousand joules—surged through the Aegis’s regenerative shunts, topping off the internal storage capacitors of the Chimera Frame Mark VI with a sharp, triumphant chime:

**[CAPACITOR CHARGE: 100% // ACCUMULATOR INTENSIFIER: 7,500 PSI READY]**

"Regenerative kinetic harvest complete," Arthur noted with clinical satisfaction. "Thank you for the electrical charge."

Arthur turned his entire focus to the six-inch steam header.

He leveled his right arm, aligning the hexagonal biogenic tungsten chisel of the **Chimera Frame Mark VI** directly against the flanged union where the high-pressure steam pipe joined the left hydraulic walking cylinder.

The vanadium-alloyed polycrystalline diamond compact (PDC) pyramid tip gleamed beneath the glowing soot clouds.

Arthur took a half-breath, locking his hip gimbals into the chassis.

He squeezed the dual-stage tandem trigger.

*KABOOOOOM!*

The Mark VI’s tandem hydraulic-pneumatic intensifier discharged with the concussive fury of a naval deck gun.

A reservoir of seven thousand five hundred pounds per square inch of hydraulic fluid expanded behind the compound piston, driving the diamond-vanadium chisel forward with thirty thousand and seventy-five pounds of concentrated kinetic thrust.

The contact shear stress at the pyramid tip exceeded two hundred and twenty thousand pounds per square inch:

$$\tau_{\text{shear}} = \frac{F}{A_{\text{contact}}} = \frac{30,075 \text{ lbs}}{0.136 \text{ sq in}} \approx 221,140 \text{ PSI}$$

*CRACK-SHATTER-BOOM!*

The eight one-inch flange bolts sheared instantaneously, severed as cleanly as boiled noodles. 

The six-inch steam header ruptured across its entire diameter. 

*FSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSHHHHHHHHHHHH!*

A catastrophic torrent of two-hundred-and-forty-PSI superheated steam exploded outward from the severed pipe in a blinding white geyser eighty feet wide. The sudden loss of steam pressure instantaneously starved the left walking cam’s hydraulic cylinders, dropping hydraulic line pressure from three thousand PSI to absolute zero in less than half a second.

The consequences for the two-hundred-and-ten-ton walking dreadnought were instantaneous, violent, and mathematically inevitable.

At the exact moment of steam failure, the Colossus had lifted its left thirty-foot shoe six feet into the air, transferring its entire two-hundred-and-ten-ton mass onto the single right walking shoe. 

Without hydraulic balancing pressure to complete the forward stride, the massive forty-ton left eccentric cam seized dead in its tracks.

The laws of structural mechanics took their toll:

$$\text{Torsional Shear Stress: } \tau = \frac{T \cdot r}{J}$$

The colossal rotational torque of the machine's central drive shaft sheared the emergency bronze mechanical clutches with an ear-splitting shriek of tearing metal:

*SKREEEEEECH-BANG!*

Deprived of balance, the Colossus tilted violently fifteen degrees to the left.

The thirty-foot left walking shoe dropped like a falling skyscraper, plunging into the deep, uncompacted alluvial river clay of the cornfield with an earth-shattering impact:

*KER-THUMP-CRUNCH!*

The thirty-foot steel shoe sank four feet deep into the freezing mud, burying itself up to the ankle gimbals. The entire sixty-foot superstructure groaned in mechanical agony as the right walking shoe lifted into the air, spinning uselessly on its eccentric cam like the leg of an overturned beetle.

The Colossus of Gary was permanently immobilized. Its forward advance had ended.

Aboard the command bridge sixty feet above, Baron Malakor was thrown violently against his bronze helm console, his cybernetic eye lens cracking against the armor plate.

"What happened?!" Malakor screamed through his speaking tube, blood dripping from his nose. "Why have we stopped?! Fire the starboard flail! Grind whatever is down there to hash!"

High above on the starboard flank, the operator of the forty-foot excavator flail boom swung the massive articulated steel arm around, revving its six-foot circular buzzsaw and barbed flail chains to five hundred RPM, aiming downward toward the left gimbal where Arthur stood.

The screaming buzzsaw approached Arthur’s position at seventy miles per hour, its heavy carbide teeth throwing white-hot sparks as it scraped across the Colossus’s own hull plating.

Arthur Vance did not panic.

He disengaged his magnetic boot locks, crouched low against the tilted steel hull, and activated the **High-Pressure Pneumatic Jump Thrusters** on his hips.

*KER-BLAST!*

The eight-thousand-PSI nitrogen gas springs discharged with an explosive pop.

Arthur launched fifteen feet straight up the tilted vertical armor of the Colossus, his greatcoat flying behind him, soaring cleanly over the screaming buzzsaw blade as it struck the gimbal where he had stood a fraction of a second before:

*SPARK-CLANG-SHATTER!*

Mid-flight, Arthur pulled a pressurized canister of cryogenic tetrafluoroethane from his tactical harness.

As he crested above the excavator arm’s primary hydraulic pivot gimbal, Arthur depressed the aerosol release, directing a concentrated stream of sub-zero refrigerant directly into the rotating bronze bearing race:

"Thermal shock embrittlement," Arthur noted aloud as he fell toward the excavator boom.

Minus fifteen degrees Fahrenheit collided with four-hundred-degree friction-heated bronze. The sudden thermal contraction shattered the bronze bearing cage in a web of brittle micro-fractures.

Arthur landed lightly atop the excavator boom’s steel box girder, his magnetic boots clamping instantly to the metal deck.

Without breaking stride, Arthur raised his right arm, driving the Chimera Frame Mark VI’s diamond chisel straight into the fractured pivot pin.

*KABOOOOM!*

Thirty thousand pounds of hydraulic force punched through the brittle bronze pin.

The four-inch steel hinge bolt sheared in two. 

With a deafening groan of severed hydraulic hoses and tearing steel cables, the colossal forty-foot flail arm tore free from its mountings, tumbling sixty feet down through the air before crashing into the snow-covered field below with a cataclysmic thud that sent snow flying fifty feet into the air.

The Gary Reaper infantry watching from the perimeter trenches gasped in paralyzed, frozen terror.

"The arm... he broke the flail with his bare hand!" a raider captain screamed, throwing his rifle into the mud. "The Ghost is tearing the Colossus apart!"

Arthur Vance walked calmly up the forty-degree slope of the tilted engine deck, stepping over severed steam conduits and hissing relief valves.

He reached the armored hatch of the command cupola. With a single pneumatic strike of the Mark VI’s chisel, Arthur punched through the heavy padlock, kicking the steel door wide open.

Inside the cramped, smoke-filled bridge, illuminated only by red emergency lamps and sputtering oil gauges, stood **Baron Malakor**.

The warlord was breathing heavily, his chrome breastplate dented and his scarred face twisted in a mixture of feral rage and overwhelming dread. He gripped a heavy sixty-pound pneumatic demolition sledgehammer, raising it over his shoulder as Arthur stepped through the hatch.

"Stay back, you bookkeeper demon!" Malakor snarled, his voice trembling. "I forged this Barony from twenty years of blood and iron! You cannot take my world with a pencil!"

Malakor swung the sixty-pound pneumatic sledgehammer with all his remaining strength, aiming directly at Arthur’s head.

Arthur did not flinch.

He raised his left forearm, catching the head of the sledgehammer cleanly against the **Sovereign Aegis buckler**.

*CLANG-SHOCK!*

The electromagnetic induction coils absorbed the blow instantly. Arthur’s right hand shot forward, the Mark VI’s hydraulic gripper clamping onto the tool’s pneumatic feed hose.

With a two-hundred-pound twist, Arthur ripped the hose free. 

The heavy tool depressurized with a dying gasp of air, dropping uselessly from Malakor’s exhausted hands to clatter against the deck plates.

Before Malakor could draw a sidearm, Arthur stepped into the warlord’s chest, planting his mechanical palm against Malakor’s breastplate. The Mark VI applied exactly two hundred pounds of non-lethal hydraulic pinning force, driving the warlord back into his command chair and locking him in place.

Arthur pulled his gold pocket watch from his waistcoat, clicked the hunter case open, and checked the second hand.

*5:27:14 PM.*

"Total elapsed dismantling time: three minutes and thirty-two seconds," Arthur stated, his voice calm, dry, and terrifyingly absolute. "Your Colossus is demobilized. Your flail arm is destroyed. Your steam pressure is zero. The engagement is concluded."

Arthur turned to the boiler control panel, methodically pulling the emergency cold-water injector levers and opening the main superheater chimney bypasses. 

The violent roaring of the twin boilers died down into a soft, harmless hiss of expanding steam, preventing any risk of a catastrophic boiler explosion.

Arthur stepped out onto the open bridge wing of the immobilized Colossus, looking down across the snowy fields.

Beside the railhead, Eleanor Vance stood with Captain Cross and the soldiers of Company B. Eleanor’s golden 528 Hz starlight flared upward in a warm, welcoming beacon of peace that illuminated the entire valley.

Seeing their warlord pinned and their invincible walking juggernaut completely neutralized, the five hundred Gary Reapers outside dropped their weapons into the snowdrifts, falling to their knees in unconditional surrender.

Arthur Vance opened his leather ledger, pulled his mechanical pencil, and inscribed the final balance on page eighty-four:

*Asset Dismantled: 'Colossus of Gary' (210-Ton Bucyrus-Erie Conversion).*  
*Status: Drive cams seized. Primary steam header sheared. Flail assembly demounted.*  
*Casualties: 0 Fatalities.*  
*Iron Barony Hostile Assets: 100% Neutralized.*

"The Colossus is broken," Arthur whispered into his radio channel. "Bring up the transport sleds. We take custody of the Iron Barony."
