# Chapter 73: The Tenth Sovereign Hub

At twelve o'clock noon on the day of the winter solstice, the sun crested the jagged granite spires of the Grand Teton range.

*Day 18 // 12:00 PM.*

There was no cataclysmic roar of tearing bedrock. There was no two-thousand-cubic-kilometer phreatomagmatic ash plume blotting out the sun, nor did rivers of incandescent pyroclastic death sweep down the Snake River Canyon to bury the settlements of the West.

Instead, the winter sky above the Yellowstone plateau was a flawless, piercing vault of crystalline cobalt blue. Sunlight sparkled off forty feet of pristine mountain snowpacks, illuminating the ancient caldera not as a tomb of smoldering cinder, but as a breathtaking, peaceful sanctuary of sleeping geological power.

Standing on the surface observation platform of Project Tartarus, three hundred feet above the snowbound plateau, Master Engineer Naomi Tsosie pulled off her heavy welding goggles and looked out across the vast valley.

Beside her stood Arthur Vance, his charcoal-wool archivist waistcoat buttoned neatly over his linen shirt, his gold pocket watch resting securely in his left vest pocket. Over his right shoulder, the **Chimera Frame Mark V** stood docked in its portable brass transport rig, its accumulator gauges resting at an idle four thousand PSI.

"Solstice noon transit confirmed," Arthur announced, checking his chronometer against the zenith shadow. "Solar elevation angle: twenty-two point four degrees. Zero seismic tremors detected across twenty-four regional monitoring stations. Tectonic status: absolute crustal equilibrium."

"Twelve o'clock," Naomi murmured softly, a broad, triumphant smile breaking across her wind-chapped face. "Caldwell’s apocalyptic deadline has come and gone. The world didn't burn."

"A well-maintained machine does not burn simply because a cultist demands it," Arthur noted, pulling his leather ledger and mechanical pencil from his pocket. "It merely requires competent torque application, clean contact points, and the elimination of parasitic electrical resistance."

In the valley below, the transformation of the subterranean facility was already proceeding with clockwork efficiency.

Under the joint supervision of Naomi Tsosie, Captain Rachel Cross, and the veteran engineers of Company B, the sinister military complex formerly known as Project Tartarus was being systematically dismantled, purged of Covenant iconography, and re-engineered into the foundation of a permanent sovereign civilization.

In the lower generating galleries of the complex, Shoshone roughnecks and Confederation mechanics were completing the overhaul of two colossal pre-Collapse Parsons reaction steam turbines. 

Deep beneath the caprock, clean, supercritical geothermal steam—purged of sulfuric acid and corrosive chlorides by Eleanor’s 528 Hz starlight field—was routed through forged star-iron steam headers into the high-pressure turbine casings.

Naomi led Arthur down the spiral iron staircase into the main generating hall, pointing to the massive vibration-damped tachometer dials mounted to the generator bulkheads.

"Report on turbine synchronization, Master Tsosie," Arthur directed.

Naomi tapped the brass instrument bezel with pride. "Turbine Set Alpha and Turbine Set Beta are both spinning at thirty-six hundred RPM. Steam inlet temperature: eight hundred and eighty-five degrees Fahrenheit. Inlet pressure: three thousand two hundred PSI. Back-pressure condensing vacuum: zero point seven five PSI absolute."

Arthur checked his ledger, calculating the thermodynamic efficiency of the supercritical Rankine cycle on page fifty-two:

$$\eta_{\text{thermal}} = 1 - \frac{h_2 - h_3}{h_1 - h_4}$$

$$\text{Power Output } P = \dot{m} \cdot \left(h_{\text{in}} - h_{\text{out}}\right) \cdot \eta_{\text{generator}}$$

$$\dot{m} = 1,450,000 \text{ lb/hr}, \quad \Delta h = 480 \text{ BTU/lb}, \quad \eta_{\text{gen}} = 0.96$$

$$P \approx 250.4 \text{ Megawatts Electrical}$$

"Two hundred and fifty megawatts of continuous, uninterruptible electrical power," Arthur announced, his pencil tracing the clean numbers. "Generated with zero fossil fuel consumption and zero atmospheric emissions. With Anchor Node Five operating as a stabilized thermodynamic heat sink, this facility can sustain two hundred and fifty megawatts for ten thousand consecutive years without inducing crustal cooling or seismic subsidence."

"Two hundred and fifty megawatts..." Naomi breathed, staring up at the humming star-iron generator stators. "That’s more power than the entire pre-Collapse state of Wyoming consumed in mid-winter. What are we going to do with that much energy up here at eight thousand feet?"

Arthur closed his ledger with a decisive snap.

"We will do what civilization has always done when gifted with thermodynamic abundance," Arthur answered. "We will build life."

Arthur led Naomi and Eleanor out toward the southern exposure of the plateau, where four hundred Shoshone builders, Jackson Hole homesteaders, and paroled Covenant laborers were already breaking frozen ground under the guidance of Sergeant Major Hank Bradley’s civil engineering team.

Arthur laid out the master architectural blueprint on the hood of a Shoshone tracked crawler:

"Primary civic directive: **The Yellowstone Geothermal Sanctuary**," Arthur declared. "First: District Geothermal Heating. The low-pressure steam exhaust from Turbine Sets Alpha and Beta—discharging seventy million BTU per hour of clean one-hundred-and-sixty-degree condensate—will be circulated through a closed-loop subterranean hydronic piping network embedded throughout the Jackson Hole valley. To prevent localized ground subsidence, all geothermal brine and condensed water will be returned to the deep formation through six dedicated peripheral reinjection wells at a depth of four thousand feet. The entire basin floor will maintain an artificial soil temperature of sixty-two degrees Fahrenheit, melting surface snowpacks and creating a permanent agricultural microclimate."

Naomi leaned over the blueprint, her eyes tracing the grid of green-shaded sectors. "Sub-surface geothermal greenhouses?"

"Fifty thousand square feet of multi-span polycarbonate greenhouses," Arthur confirmed. "Heated by turbine tail-water, irrigated by condensed artesian steam, and illuminated by high-efficiency luminescent phosphor tubes powered by our twenty-five-megawatt baseline. Photosynthetically active radiation will be maintained at eight hundred micromoles per square meter per second on an eighteen-hour photoperiod. While the surrounding Rockies endure minus thirty-degree blizzards, this valley will produce three continuous harvests per year of winter wheat, russet potatoes, protein-rich legumes, and vital medicinal herbs."

Beside them, Eleanor stepped forward, looking down at the growing encampment where hot food, clean water, and warm shelter were being distributed to hundreds of displaced families who had fled Covenant tyranny.

"And what of the Ashen Covenant survivors, Arthur?" Eleanor asked gently, her eyes full of compassionate concern. "Many of them were brainwashed into believing that destruction was holy."

"Eighty-four former Covenant initiates have formally surrendered their weapons," Arthur stated, consulting his personnel muster roll. "Their psychological evaluations indicate that the majority were coerced by Caldwell’s apocalyptic cult of despair or suffering from chronic sulfur-dioxide neuro-toxicity. Now that the air is purified and the geothermal wells are stabilized, they will be incorporated into the Sanctuary's industrial workforce under a structured restorative rehabilitation protocol. They will be instructed in fundamental mechanical principles: valve packings, pipe threading, steam loop thermodynamics, and heat-exchanger cleaning. Constructive labor and measurable technical mastery are the most effective treatments for ideological hysteria."

Arthur glanced toward the auxiliary machine shop, where Shoshone prosthetists and Confederation field medics were refitting **Slag-Hound Kenneth**.

His shattered silicate armor had been replaced by smooth plates of lightweight structural star-iron, and his severed thoracic hydraulic lines were being reconnected to non-combat industrial lifting linkages.

"Mr. Kenneth possesses an exceptional mechanical aptitude for high-pressure pneumatic systems," Arthur noted deadpan. "Stripped of his destructive rotary cutterheads, his eighteen-hundred-pound frame makes him an incomparable crane operator for heavy casing pipe handling. He has signed a three-year civil rehabilitation contract as Head Rig Mechanic of the Sanctuary under Naomi's personal supervision."

Naomi Tsosie let out a hearty laugh that echoed across the snowfields. "From apocalyptic monster to roughneck supervisor. Only an archivist could calculate a redemption arc based on crane lifting capacity."

"Morals are subjective, Master Tsosie," Arthur replied without cracking a smile. "Labor productivity, mechanical leverage, and boiler maintenance are absolute."

Captain Rachel Cross approached the command table, stamping snow from her heavy insulated combat boots. She unrolled a topographical rail map of the region.

"Arthur, Naomi," Cross said, pointing her grease pencil at Togwotee Pass. "We need to address strategic logistics. The Snake River Gorge railhead is currently isolated on this side of the Grand Tetons. To connect the Yellowstone Geothermal Sanctuary with the rest of the Confederation, we must complete a sixty-mile star-iron rail spur connecting through Togwotee Pass at nine thousand six hundred feet, linking directly into the Wasatch-Vulcan Junction mainline."

"The Cascade Steel Works at Vulcan Junction already has thirty miles of one-hundred-and-thirty-six-pound star-iron rail pre-staged in the rail yards," Arthur confirmed. "With Kenneth’s roughneck crews clearing the right-of-way and the Shoshone crawlers laying roadbed ballast, the Togwotee spur can achieve golden-spike completion within sixty calendar days. Once complete, twenty-car diesel tank trains from Elena Rostova’s Wasatch refinery will transit the pass weekly, trading refined kerosene and lubricants in exchange for geothermal electricity and fresh greenhouse produce."

At 1:30 PM, on a freshly cleared obsidian terrace overlooking the thermal springs, the formal civic ceremony was convened.

Surrounded by two hundred Shoshone clan leaders, Confederation soldiers of Company B, and Jackson Hole valley delegates, Arthur Vance unfurled a heavy parchment charter bearing the official embossed seal of the **Western Confederation of Sovereign Hubs**.

Arthur dipped his brass fountain pen into indelible iron-gall ink.

"By authority of the Supreme Council of the Western Confederation, and in accordance with the Archival Reconstruction Protocols established at Bunker Seven and Oakhaven," Arthur read in a clear, resonant voice:

"Be it known that Project Tartarus and the surrounding geothermal basin of Jackson Hole are hereby chartered as **The Tenth Sovereign Hub: The Yellowstone Geothermal Sanctuary**."

Arthur looked directly at Naomi Tsosie.

"Master Engineer Naomi Tsosie," Arthur said, holding out the ceremonial star-iron spanner and the embossed charter. "By unanimous recommendation of Captain Cross, Eleanor Vance, and myself, you are hereby commissioned as **Master Warden of the Tenth Sovereign Hub**. The custody of Anchor Node Five, the supercritical steam manifolds, and the defense of the high continental passes are entrusted to your leadership."

Naomi Tsosie stood straight, her chin held high and her eyes glistening with fierce, honorable pride. She accepted the star-iron spanner, gripping the heavy tool with both hands.

"I accept the charge, Arthur Vance," Naomi said, her voice ringing across the mountains. "So long as the Shoshone walk these passes, the pylon will sing at five hundred and twenty-eight Hertz, and the fire of the earth will serve the people, not destroy them."

A thunderous cheer erupted from the assembled crowd. Horns from the Shoshone tracked crawlers blasted in triumphant unison, their deep, sonorous notes echoing off the frozen face of Mount Moran and the Tetons.

Arthur signed the charter with a firm, practiced flourish. Eleanor added her signature in radiant golden script, her Sovereign starlight sealing the parchment with an indelible luminescent watermark.

At 2:15 PM, Arthur climbed to the newly installed high-altitude communications tower atop the Tartarus control bunker.

Sergeant Bradley threw the master knife switch on the long-range vacuum-tube telegraph transmitter.

*CLICK-CLACK!*

Powered by five hundred watts of clean geothermal electricity, the star-iron transmitter began pulsing high-frequency Morse code into the sky, bouncing off the ionosphere to link with the continental network:

**[TO: HUB 01 BUNKER 07 // HUB 02 OAKHAVEN // HUB 04 WASATCH REFINERY // HUB 05 MOUNT SENTINEL]**  
**[FROM: TENTH SOVEREIGN HUB // YELLOWSTONE GEOTHERMAL SANCTUARY]**  
**[CALDERA STABILIZED. ANCHOR NODE 05 LOCKED AT 528.00 HZ. 250 MW GEOTHERMAL ONLINE. THE CONTINENTAL SPINE IS SECURED.]**

Within ninety seconds, the brass sounder on the communications desk began clattering with incoming reply telegrams:

From Wasatch, Elena Rostova replied:  
**[REFINERY CONGRATULATES WARDEN TSOSIE. FIRST 50-CAR DIESEL FUEL CONVOY READY TO TRANSIT PASS.]**

From Mount Sentinel, Dr. Sean Chen transmitted:  
**[SEISMIC FLUX ACROSS ENTIRE CORDILLERA ZERO. PLANETARY LATTICE HARMONIZED. MAGNIFICENT WORK.]**

And from Oakhaven, Mayor Silas Vance messaged:  
**[TEN HUBS COMPLETE. THE FEDERATION EMERGES. RETURN TO THE DIVIDE FOR ACCORD RATIFICATION.]**

Arthur Vance tore off the paper telegraph ribbon, folded it neatly, and slipped it into his pocket ledger.

He turned toward Eleanor, who was looking eastward toward the Great Divide, where the afternoon sun cast long golden shadows across the snow.

"The Tenth Hub is established," Eleanor said softly, her eyes shining with warmth. "The foundation is indestructible."

"The foundation is secure," Arthur agreed, checking his gold pocket watch.

*2:48 PM.*

"Now we proceed to the summit of the Continental Divide to ratify the Continental Accord and complete the survey of the western world."
