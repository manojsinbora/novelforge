# Story Bible: The Omnicrafter of the Fallen Era

**Protagonist:** Arthur Vance (23) — Municipal Archivist & Battle-Artificer
**Sister:** Eleanor Vance (15) — Latent Starlight Sovereign Vessel
**Combat Edge:** Pure Encyclopedic Deduction (No magical cheat system; cross-references physics, anatomy, and metallurgy to collapse higher-tier mutant anatomies).
**Master Craft:** Modular weapon engineering ('The Chimera Frame'), bio-enhancements, kinetic runic arrays.
**The Comedy Core:** The Saitama/King paradox. Arthur pulls off genius lethal feats, but ridiculous coincidences (dropped screws, smoke clearing on a bumbling ally, or Barnaby the camp rat) rob him of credit, while mutant syndicates live in terror of 'The Ghost Artificer'.
**Progression System:** 7-Level Awakening (Novice -> Intermediate -> Master -> Grandmaster -> Great Grandmaster -> Sovereign -> Transcendent with Early -> Mid -> Late -> Peak).
