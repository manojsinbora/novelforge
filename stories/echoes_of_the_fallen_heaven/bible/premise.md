# Echoes of the Fallen Heaven — Canonical Premise

**Genre:** Progression Web Novel | Awakening Cultivation | Tactical Crafting | Deadpan Satire & Action

**Elevator Pitch:** *One-Punch Man* meets *The Legendary Mechanic* in a ruthless awakening cultivation sect.

**Core Premise:** 
Lin Chen, an ancient grandmaster regressed 300 years into his 16-year-old mortal body, must survive the impending subterranean apocalypse. Rather than relying solely on traditional pill-swallowing alchemy, Lin Chen dominates using **wits, bio-enhancements, meridian grafting, formation energy inversion, and modular mechanical weaponry**.

**The Comedy Spine ("The Saitama / King Paradox"):**
Lin Chen builds ingenious tactical traps, customized needle-arrays, and bio-alchemical triggers that effortlessly dispatch opponents leagues above his realm. However, absurd coincidences and misunderstandings always rob him of the glory:
- Incompetent elders (like Elder Han) or haughty rivals (like Song Yu) unwittingly arrive just in time to take full public credit for the victories.
- The sect hierarchy celebrates the "miraculous heroics" of frauds, while Lin Chen watches with dry, deadpan exhaustion, quietly taking the actual material loot and pill resources to cultivate in peace.

**The 7-Tier Progression Ladder:**
1. **Novice** (Early -> Mid -> Late -> Peak)
2. **Intermediate** (Early -> Mid -> Late -> Peak)
3. **Master** (Early -> Mid -> Late -> Peak)
4. **Grand Master** (Early -> Mid -> Late -> Peak)
5. **Great Grand Master** (Early -> Mid -> Late -> Peak)
6. **Sovereign** (Early -> Mid -> Late -> Peak)
7. **Transcendent** (Early -> Mid -> Late -> Peak)

**Primary Objectives:**
1. Purify and graft sister Lin Xia's sovereign meridians before her constitution detonates.
2. Upgrade his own mortal vessel through bio-modifications and hidden kinetic arrays.
3. Expose the traitorous sect elders without blowing his low-profile cover.
4. Prepare the Nine Peaks for the subterranean Void Leviathan breach.
