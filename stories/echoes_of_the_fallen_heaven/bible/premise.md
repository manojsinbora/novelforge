# Echoes of the Fallen Heaven — Premise

**Elevator Pitch:** Return of the Mount Hua Sect meets The Legendary Mechanic in a grimdark cultivation apocalypse.

**Core Premise:** Regressed grandmaster Lin Chen navigates sect politics and subterranean monster incursions.

**Central Conflict:** Verdant Cloud Sect vs Blood Raven Warlords vs Subterranean Primordial Leviathans.

**Protagonist Objective:** Protect his sister Lin Xia, purge corrupt sect elders, and seal the ancient planar veins.

**Primary Antagonist:** Patriarch Yan of the Blood Raven Sect & The Shadow Sovereign.
